# Historical Slack scenario census

This file preserves the causal lessons from recent Chickpea product work without copying private task history into the repository. It is a planning index, not an executable suite. The typed live catalog remains the only source of executable contracts.

The review covered 24 recent Chickpea task histories, the Agent-first acceptance and Agent-authoring runbooks, the synthetic authoring corpus, and the live-contract plan appendix. Private working notes for this review account for which candidate IDs came from each source. Public rows below combine duplicate conversations around the same product lesson. Sources that only repeated a lesson use `duplicate` in those notes; sources without a reusable product lesson use `no_new_lesson`. Neither disposition needs a public row.

Dispositions are intentionally simple:

- `v1_live` means the candidate belongs in the named live contract or variant. A pending contract remains non-executable until its typed case exists.
- `deterministic_only` means protocol, language, replay, or denial behavior belongs in local deterministic coverage rather than a scored live mutation.
- `backlog` means useful live coverage deferred until the first complete deep pass works reliably.

## Promotion rule

A future session may promote a `backlog` candidate into the typed manifest without changing its candidate ID only after it defines:

- at least one authoritative observer for every scored assertion;
- the exact actor and fixture requirements;
- each mutation's fixture class;
- exact reversal or attributed-residue behavior; and
- happy-path, permission-denial, ambiguous-write, historical-regression, and cleanup coverage appropriate to the case.

Before publication, compare newly edited rows with the private source register and repeat the public export privacy scan. Keep task identifiers, task titles, Slack coordinates, screenshots, personal or customer data, credentials, internal URLs, and private implementation details outside Git.
